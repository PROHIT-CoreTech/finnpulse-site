The Role, Explained

What Does a Fractional CFO Actually Do?

Senior Financial Leadership On-Demand

A Fractional CFO gives your business access to senior-level financial thinking, structure, and strategic governance — without the cost of hiring a full-time executive.

  • ✓ Financial Control
  • ✓ MIS & BI
  • ✓ Strategic Growth

Scope of Work

The Six Areas We Manage

  1. 01

    Financial Control

    Make your numbers reliable.

  2. 02

    MIS & Business Intelligence

    Turn financial data into management insights.

  3. 03

    Cash & Working Capital

    Know where your cash is today — and where it will be 13 weeks from now.

  4. 04

    Budgeting & Forecasting

    Plan your growth in numbers.

  5. 05

    Systems & Automation

    Build processes that don’t depend on people remembering what to do.

  6. 06

    Profitability & Strategic Finance

    Understand where you make money and where you don’t.
